A convenient tool for RFIC, RFID, NFC and WPT researchers. This estimator considers rectangular planar spiral coils with an aspect ratio of up to 4.0 and a cross-sectional aspect ratio of height to width not exceeding unity. It is based on physical principles, hence scalable and valid for any dimension and inductance range.

Notes

The formula lowers the overall maximum error from 28% down to 5.6% compared to the FastHenry2 estimator.

This estimation is primarily based on the formulae devised by H. A. Aebischer in Inductance Formula for Rectangular Planar Spiral Inductors with Rectangular Conductor Cross Section, with required alterations regarding the geometry of rectangular planar coils and their physical structure in PCB implementation made by S. Chatterjee, School of Physics, ITMO University, Saint Petersburg, Russian Federation.

Valid designs: aspect ratio A/B up to 4.0, cross-section h/s ≤ 1, and geometric fill ratio within the published limits. Invalid combinations that would cause overlapping turns are rejected.
